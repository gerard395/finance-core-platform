# PC-002 – Transactional Purchase Credit Posting & Tax Reversal

## Contract

`PostPurchaseCreditInvoice` posts only a `Finalized` purchase credit and accepts an explicit accounting `PostingDate` plus the acting domain `UserId`. A successful outer database transaction creates one balanced reversal journal entry, one tax reversal per credit line, one positive `Payable/Debit` open item without due date, one durable claim per source purchase line, one posting linkage, and the `PostedBy`/`PostedAt` transition. A repeated post is idempotent.

The reversal uses immutable historical facts only: the expense/asset account snapshot per source line, the exact Input VAT journal line/account referenced by the original `TaxPosting`, the AP control account and relation from the source `Payable/Credit`, and the source posting's purchase journal. Current purchase configuration and later active/inactive state are ignored. Positive VAT creates a VAT credit line; zero-rated, exempt and outside-scope facts retain their classification and create a reversal `TaxPosting` without a fictitious VAT journal line. Fiscal reporting uses the credit's `FiscalReportingDate`; the journal uses the explicit `PostingDate`.

## Atomicity and concurrency

Locks are acquired in the shared order document → source invoice → source payable open item. Credit lines are processed by ascending source-line identity. Database uniqueness on `(AdministrationId, SourcePurchaseInvoiceLineId)` selects exactly one winner when credits compete; the loser receives `SourceLineAlreadyCredited` and remains `Finalized`. All claims are transaction-local until the complete posting commits. Any journal, tax, open-item, claim, linkage or status failure rolls back every fact.

## PC-003 boundary

PC-002 creates neither `OpenItemMatch` nor `OpenItemSettlement`; source and credit balances therefore coexist. The tenant-scoped posting read model exposes the credit/source invoice identities, both payable identities, posting date, journal entry, positive gross amount, reversal tax identities and claim completeness. PC-003 must re-read current balances, matches and settlements under the same open-item locks before matching.
