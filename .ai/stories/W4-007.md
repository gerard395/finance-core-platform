# W4-007 – Transactional Sales Invoice Posting

## Inventory and boundaries

- `SalesInvoice` persists immutable customer, Relation, address, currency, line and tax snapshots. Only `SalesInvoice::post()` permits `Finalized → Posted`.
- `SalesInvoicePostingSource` adds the narrow tenant-scoped locked load required by this use case. Its Eloquent implementation queries `(administration_id, id)` with `FOR UPDATE` and hydrates persisted invoice truth.
- W4-007A0 supplies readiness through `SalesPostingConfigurationReader` and append-only idempotency/traceability through `SalesInvoicePostingRepository`.
- `SalesTaxSnapshot::forCalculation()` reconstructs calculation input from the finalized snapshot. Posting does not call a current TaxCode, Customer, Relation or Address reader.
- `PostSalesInvoiceWithTax`, `CreateSalesInvoicePostingRequest` and `PostingEngine` remain the owners of net/tax/gross and balanced debit/credit construction. The request factory now also accepts the already calculated fiscal lines so fiscal posting does not duplicate request assembly.
- JournalEntry, TaxPosting and OpenItem stores, linkage, locked invoice load and invoice update all use the ambient Laravel database connection. Their nested transaction calls are savepoints inside the use-case-owned outer transaction; none commits autonomously.
- No migration is needed. W4-007A00/W4-007A0 already provide all ownership, FK and unique constraints.

## Transaction owner and order

`PostSalesInvoice` accepts only `AdministrationId` and `SalesInvoiceId` and owns this order:

1. begin outer transaction;
2. tenant-scoped `FOR UPDATE` invoice load;
3. read append-only linkage;
4. enforce Finalized/idempotency/inconsistency preconditions;
5. resolve the four explicit posting-configuration identities;
6. construct fiscal inputs from persisted line tax snapshots and generate fresh typed identities;
7. construct a balanced posted JournalEntry and immutable TaxPostings through the existing fiscal orchestration and PostingEngine;
8. append JournalEntry;
9. append each TaxPosting;
10. append one Receivable OpenItem using snapshot RelationId, exact AR gross, invoice date and JournalEntry source;
11. append authoritative SalesInvoice → JournalEntry/OpenItem linkage;
12. call `SalesInvoice::post()` and persist the aggregate through `SalesInvoiceUpdater`;
13. commit.

Any exception, duplicate identity, store failure, linkage conflict or updater failure escapes the transaction callback and rolls back every write. The public use case returns typed `PostingFailure` without SQL details.

## Preconditions and idempotency

- Unknown/cross-tenant invoice: `NotFound`.
- Draft or Cancelled: `InvalidState`.
- Finalized without linkage: eligible to post.
- Posted/Paid with linkage: `AlreadyPosted`; no writes or status mutation.
- Posted/Paid without linkage: `FinancialStateInconsistent`; never repaired by reposting.
- Finalized with linkage: `FinancialStateInconsistent`; never repaired or overwritten.
- Missing/invalid configuration: `ConfigurationMissing`/`ConfigurationInvalid`; no writes.

The row lock serializes attempts for one invoice. The linkage composite primary key remains the hard database idempotency boundary. A real two-process MySQL test proves one `Success`, one `AlreadyPosted`, one JournalEntry, one Receivable OpenItem, one logical TaxPosting set, one linkage and a Posted invoice.

## Financial facts and historical independence

The configured Sales Journal, AR, Revenue and Output VAT IDs are used exactly; no account/journal heuristic exists. Journal posting date and OpenItem opening date are the immutable invoice date. The AR debit is the exact gross produced by the existing snapshot-driven tax calculation and becomes the OpenItem original amount without floats or extra rounding.

TaxPostings retain immutable invoice, line, JournalEntry and tax snapshot traceability. Changing/deactivating the current TaxCode after finalization does not affect posting. Renaming the Relation or deactivating the Customer likewise does not affect posting; the factual snapshot RelationId remains the Receivable owner. Zero-rate invoices retain a zero TaxPosting without a fictitious VAT journal line. Multiple tax snapshots create their existing per-line TaxPostings while producing one balanced JournalEntry and one gross OpenItem.

This use case ends at `Finalized → Posted`. It does not mark Paid, create settlements/payments/bank transactions, mutate a source Order, allocate invoice quantities, or expose routes, buttons, Blade or navigation. Those remain later Banking, W4-007A and W4-008 responsibilities.
