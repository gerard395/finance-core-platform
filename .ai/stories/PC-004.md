# PC-004 – Purchase Credit Review & Regression

## Review result

The complete PC-000 through PC-003 chain is reviewed and merge-ready. Purchase credits remain tenant-owned, EUR-only, source-bound documents with immutable historical supplier/address/account/tax facts and complete source-line reversals only. Lifecycle, external identity, posted claims, historical journal/net/VAT/AP reversal, zero-tax behavior, positive `Payable/Debit`, automatic source matching and independent Web permissions retain their documented boundaries.

The review found and fixed one correctness issue: automatic matching used the open balance as of the user-supplied `PostingDate` instead of the current durable balance required by the PC contract. A durable settlement with a later effective date could consequently be ignored. Posting now reloads and uses each locked OpenItem's full current open amount; a regression test proves that a later-dated settlement of 40 limits a 121 credit match to 81. The match policy still validates identities, date ordering, side, type, relation, currency and available amount.

Broad Purchasing, Accounting/OpenItem, Banking/B2, SalesCredit, authorization, Web/security and real-MySQL concurrency regressions pass. The database review confirms migrations 000054/000055 are additive, tenant-safe and protected by same-tenant RESTRICT foreign keys and uniqueness. No persistent automated test data, duplicate facts, orphan facts or negative open balances remain.

Deferred scope remains unchanged: partial PurchaseCredits, supplier refunds, payment reversal, attachments/OCR, Purchase Orders, International Purchase VAT, Bank Import and VAT/ICP reporting.
