# W1-003B0 – JournalEntry Reconstitution Contract

## Inventory

`JournalEntry` owns immutable `JournalEntryId`, `AdministrationId`, `JournalId`, `PostingDate` and `JournalEntryReference`; only `JournalEntryStatus` changes. Its child collection is keyed by immutable `JournalEntryLineId`. `addLine()` rejects duplicate identity and both add/remove are Draft-only. `post()` changes the only other status, Draft, to Posted; the enum contains no further statuses. Posted entries must remain immutable after hydration.

`JournalEntryLine` is readonly and requires ID, LedgerAccountId, exactly one non-negative debit/credit `Money`, and description. Money carries exact string amount and Currency. The line constructor owns debit/credit shape and sign invariants.

The entity itself exposes no balance API. Creation of financial truth runs through `PostingEngine`: `PostingValidation` requires at least two lines, unique line IDs, one Currency and equal exact debit/credit totals; only a valid request is assembled as Draft and posted. `PostingResult` exposes either that Posted entry or validation errors. Both Draft and Posted are factual states accepted by the existing constructor.

## Reconstitution contract

`JournalEntry::reconstitute(...)` receives the complete typed persisted snapshot: entry ID, Administration ID, Journal ID, posting date, reference, final enum status and a list of already-valid `JournalEntryLine` entities. It indexes the children directly inside the aggregate and assigns final status through construction. It calls neither `addLine()` nor `post()`, has no events or Infrastructure concepts, and uses no reflection or setters.

All snapshots reject duplicate child identity and mixed Currency. Because Draft is factually mutable and may be incomplete, it is not required to be balanced or contain two lines. Posted snapshots reuse `PostingValidation` through a `PostingRequest`, rejecting incomplete or unbalanced persisted financial truth without duplicating posting arithmetic. The individual line constructor continues to reject invalid debit/credit shape and negative amounts.

Normal business creation remains unchanged: callers construct Draft state, `PostingEngine` validates, adds lines and posts it. Reconstitution is only for persistence hydration (and a future import of already-existing factual state), never a second posting route.

## Verification scope

Unit coverage restores Draft and Posted entries with exact immutable context, IDs, line order, debit/credit Money and Currency. It proves Draft remains mutable, Posted remains immutable, duplicate IDs and mixed Currency fail, and unbalanced Posted state fails through existing validation. Existing PostingEngine tests protect the unchanged creation lifecycle.
