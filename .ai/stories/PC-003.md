# PC-003 – Purchase Credit Matching & Web Flow

## Automatic matching

Purchase-credit posting and automatic source matching form one outer transaction. After the reversal entry, tax facts and positive `Payable/Debit` exist, the use case locks the credit and explicit source `Payable/Credit` pair in lexicographic OpenItem identity order and reloads settlements and matches. The applied amount is `min(current source open, current credit open)`. A positive amount creates one `OpenItemMatch` traced to both OpenItems and the credit JournalEntry; zero creates no row. Any matching failure rolls back journal, tax reversals, OpenItem, claims, linkage and Posted transition.

A fully open 121 source and 121 credit match 121 and both close. With an immutable cash settlement of 40, the match is 81 and a Debit supplier-credit balance of 40 remains. With settlement 121, no match is created and the entire 121 remains as supplier credit. Credit matching never creates or reverses `OpenItemSettlement`, a payment, refund or bank receipt.

## Concurrency

The shared order is own document → source invoice where applicable → involved OpenItems sorted by identity → settlement/match history. SupplierPayment and PurchaseCredit both recompute availability under the same OpenItem lock. Real MySQL races for payment 121 and 40 prove settlement plus match never exceeds the source original: payment may win and credit matches only the remainder, or credit may win and payment receives `AllocationExceedsOpenBalance`. Duplicate credit post and competing-credit source claims retain their PC-002 outcomes.

## Web flow

`PURCHASING.VIEW` exposes Inkoop → Creditnota's list/detail and tenant-owned Posted-EUR source selection. `PURCHASING.CREDITS_DRAFT_MANAGE`, `PURCHASING.CREDITS_FINALIZE` and `PURCHASING.CREDITS_POST` independently guard Draft create/edit/cancel, Finalize and explicit-date Post. A Posted purchase invoice links into the same create flow. Users select only complete source lines; supplier, relation, currency, account, VAT and amounts remain server-owned historical facts. Posted detail presents JournalEntry, matched amount and both remaining balances from the tenant-scoped Application read model.

Partial source-line credits, refund/payment reversal, PDF/e-mail, attachments/OCR and international VAT remain outside PC. PC-004 performs the final batch review and broader regression audit.
